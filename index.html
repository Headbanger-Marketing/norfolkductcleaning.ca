Questions About Your Norfolk Request

For commercial duct cleaning in Norfolk, how should I inspect rooftop equipment before pricing?

If the building uses rooftop units or make-up air equipment, ask for an inspection of those systems before accepting a price. Equipment count, access, condition, and duct configuration can change the scope. Confirm who arranges roof access and whether any units or areas are excluded from the proposal.

How should I plan around seasonal occupancy at my Norfolk property?

If a cottage sits unused for part of the year, tell the service provider when it is occupied and whether the system has been off. A farmhouse or year-round home may have different access and operating conditions. Ask what preparation is needed, which openings must be accessible, and whether any damaged or unusual parts fall outside cleaning.

For duct sanitizing in Norfolk, how should I describe seasonal odours first?

If a property has been closed for a season, report when the odour begins and whether it fades after ventilation. Also mention any known water, pest, or pet residue. Those details can change whether duct cleaning, source investigation, or another step is appropriate before a treatment is considered.

What should I ask about how the HRV core is serviced in Norfolk?

If the unit has a removable heat-recovery core, ask whether the work includes removal, cleaning, inspection, and correct resealing. Core materials and manufacturer instructions affect suitable handling. Photograph the model label and note any damaged seals so the proposed method and any limits are clear before the unit is opened.

For duct sealing repair in Norfolk, how should I ask for a leak map before repairs?

Request an inspection summary that identifies suspected leaks by location before work begins. A map lets you compare the proposed sealing points with visible joints and understand why each was selected. Ask how inaccessible sections will be treated in the scope and whether findings will be documented with photographs.
